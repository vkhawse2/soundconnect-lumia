SOUND CONNECT - SIMPLE INSTALL (from Downloads, 2 taps)
=========================================================

You need TWO files (they come together from the build):
  1. SoundConnect.cer          (the key)
  2. SoundConnect_... .appxbundle   (the app - ALL helper files are INSIDE it)

STEPS
-----
1. Phone: Settings > Update & security > For developers > Developer Mode ON
   (you already did this for VLC)

2. Copy BOTH files to the phone's Downloads folder (USB cable is fine).

3. Open File Explorer on the phone > Downloads.
   Tap  SoundConnect.cer  >  Install.

4. Tap the  .appxbundle  file  >  Install.
   Wait for it to finish. Done!

5. "Sound Connect" is now in your app list, with your logo.

6. Pair the WH-1000XM4 in Settings > Bluetooth, open Sound Connect,
   pick the headphones, tap Connect.

NO dependency files to add one-by-one - the bundle carries them inside.
That also means the 0x80073CF3 error from the VLC/Live Store attempt
cannot happen with this file.

If step 4 shows an error, take a photo of it and send it.
